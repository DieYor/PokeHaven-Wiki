Quests

PokeHaven EU ships a full FTB Quests book — First Steps, badge trackers, Server Life, region exploration, and a Prestige Ladder ending in Gotta Catch Em All (1025). Open with O.

Contents

    Important

    First Steps IDs never change. If you already finished starter → catch → claim → Brock, that progress stays. New chapters unlock beside it — they do not wipe the tree.

    How to use the book

    1. Press O to open the quest book (rebind under Esc → Options → Controls → FTB Quests).
    2. Start in First Steps — most tasks auto-complete; the claim quest needs the green check after you claimed.
    3. After Brock: claim the Boulder Badge in Kanto Gyms, then fight Misty. Each gym is defeat → claim badge.
    4. Side Paths and Server Life never block the gym storyline — mix them with league progress.

    Badge trackers

    • Every gym fight is followed by Claim the … Badge (checkmark).
    • Milestones at 4/8 and all 8 badges per region.
    • Elite Four unlocks only after all 8 badges for that region.
    • Kanto starts by claiming Boulder Badge from Brock (First Steps), then Misty.

    Region exploration

    Each league has an Exploration chapter: BlueMap, cartography, named waystone pins, road to the first leader, a region catch, and a landmark check. Use BlueMap + gym maps — pins should be renamed clearly (e.g. Kanto-Misty, Johto-Hub).

    Prestige Ladder & Gotta Catch Em All

    After the Sinnoh Champion, open Prestige Ladder:

    • Dex registers: 400 → 500 → 600 → 700 → 800 → 851 (full models) → 1025 Gotta Catch Em All
    • Also: 5 shinies, 50 raid wins, 50 eggs, 500 catches
    • Capstone: PokeHaven Prestige Champion

    Season rules / leaderboard: Prestige season.

    How quests relate to the rest of the game

    • Gym maps & level cap still decide where you can fight — quests track the same path, they do not replace maps. See Gym maps · Level cap.
    • Advancements (L) are a separate toast checklist. Use both if you like; neither is required to play. Achievements.
    • Rewards are helpful items (balls, heals, materials) — not pay-to-win ranks.

    Common questions

    • Book won’t open? Check Controls for “Quests” / FTB Quests — default is O.
    • Claim quest stuck? Claim with U, then click the green check on the quest. Guide: Claims.
    • Misty locked? Finish Defeat Brock, then claim Boulder Badge in Kanto Gyms.
    • Elite Four locked? Finish all 8 badges for that region (not only the last gym fight).
    • Lost progress after update? First Steps IDs are frozen on purpose. If something else looks wrong, Discord #tickets with a screenshot of the book.
