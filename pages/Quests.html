Quests

PokeHaven EU ships a custom Quest Journal (backed by FTB Quests data) — guided First Steps, every gym through Sinnoh, plus crafting, breeding, raids, Pokédex, and legend goals. Open it with O.

Contents

    Important

    First Steps IDs never change. If you already finished starter → catch → claim → Brock, that progress stays. New chapters unlock beside it — they do not wipe the tree.

    How to use the journal

    1. Press O to open the PokeHaven Quest Journal (rebind under Esc → Options → Controls → search Quests).
    2. Start in First Steps — most tasks auto-complete. When a quest is ready to claim, look for a green ! on the chapter and the green Claim buttons.
    3. After Brock, Kanto Gyms unlocks with Misty. Follow gym maps as usual.
    4. Side chapters (Settling In, Trainer Craft, …) stay available so you can mix base-building with the league path.
    Ready to claim
    Quests with unclaimed rewards sort to the top of the list and tint green. Chapters and chapter groups show a green ! when something inside is claimable. Use the Ready filter to see only claimables.
    Pinned quests & wiki links
    Click Pin on a quest to track it — a small tracker sits near the bottom-right of your HUD. Descriptions often include clickable wiki links (open in your browser). There is no floating 3D arrow in the world.

    How quests relate to the rest of the game

    • Gym maps & level cap still decide where you can fight — quests track the same path, they do not replace maps. See Gym maps · Level cap.
    • Advancements (L) are a separate toast checklist. Use both if you like; neither is required to play. Achievements.
    • Rewards are helpful items (balls, heals, materials) — not pay-to-win ranks.

    Common questions

    • Journal won’t open? Check Controls for “Quests” — default is O. You should see the PokeHaven journal, not the old FTB Quests screen.
    • Can’t find rewards? Look for green ! on chapters, the Ready filter, and the Claim / Claim all buttons in the footer.
    • Land claim quest stuck? Claim chunks with U, then claim the quest reward in the journal. Guide: Claims.
    • Misty locked? Finish Defeat Brock in First Steps first.
    • Lost progress after update? First Steps IDs are frozen on purpose. If something else looks wrong, Discord #tickets with a screenshot of the journal.
