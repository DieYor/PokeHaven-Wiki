Sandra

Sandra — Dragon specialist on PokeHaven EU.

Contents

    Walkthrough — Alfredo to Sandra

    Prepare

    Ice and Fairy punish Dragon. This gym tips toward the Nether — fire resist and a Nether waystone first.

    • Coverage: Ice and Fairy punish Dragon; Rock/Electric help into some fliers.
    • Leader party levels: about 70–70
    • Approx cap while this fight is next: ~75 — Level cap
    • Heals, status cures, spare balls, claimed base / waystone to retreat to
    • Soul Sand Valley (Nether) tip — fire resist, Nether waystone, then the gym.

    Craft the map

    1. Search Sandra in REI and craft Pearl Choker.
    2. Craft a fresh Empty Map.
    3. Combine Empty Map + Pearl Choker in the Johto Cartography Table (or trade a Map Guide villager).
    4. Hover the finished map for coordinates. Details: Gym maps.
    Important

    Do not open the Empty Map in the world first — that ruins it for gym crafting. Use the correct region cartography table.

    Fight tips

    Nether prep first, gym second. Half a party of Dragons will bounce off her Dragon core.

    1. Travel with heals; activate waystones on the route.
    2. Clear gym trainers if you need XP or PokéDollars.
    3. Heal, then challenge Sandra.
    4. Win → level cap rises → next: Pino.
