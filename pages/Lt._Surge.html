Lt. Surge

Lt. Surge — Electric specialist on PokeHaven EU.

Contents

    Walkthrough — Misty to Lt. Surge

    Prepare

    Ground types shine. Watch for speed and paralysis.

    • Coverage: Ground answers Electric best. Bulky Waters can help if they survive the first hit.
    • Leader party levels: about 32–35
    • Approx cap while this fight is next: ~40 — Level cap
    • Heals, status cures, spare balls, claimed base / waystone to retreat to
    • Savanna Plateau tip — bring food for a longer hike.

    Craft the map

    1. Search Lt. Surge in REI and craft Lieutenant Medal.
    2. Craft a fresh Empty Map.
    3. Combine Empty Map + Lieutenant Medal in the Kanto Cartography Table (or trade a Map Guide villager).
    4. Hover the finished map for coordinates. Details: Gym maps.
    Important

    Do not open the Empty Map in the world first — that ruins it for gym crafting. Use the correct region cartography table.

    Fight tips

    Paralysis and speed snowball. Pack status cures and a Ground pivot.

    1. Travel with heals; activate waystones on the route.
    2. Clear gym trainers if you need XP or PokéDollars.
    3. Heal, then challenge Lt. Surge.
    4. Win → level cap rises → next: Erika.
