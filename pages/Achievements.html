Achievements

In-game you have two main Pokémon advancement tabs: CobbleVerse (69 gym / league / legendary goals) and Cobblemon (65 catching, farming, fossils…). Vanilla Minecraft tabs also exist. This page lists both Pokémon trees.

Contents

    How to open

    1. Press L (default Advancements key), or Pause → Advancements.
    2. Switch tabs at the top — Paginated Advancements pins CobbleVerse; the Cobblemon tab is the bigger mod tree.
    3. Use toasts as a checklist. Gym maps + level cap remain your real progression.
    Important

    Coverage: CobbleVerse pack advancements + PokeHaven EU Johto/Hoenn/Sinnoh toast trees, plus every Cobblemon display advancement (catching / farming / fossils / battle). We skip Cobblemon recipe-book unlock spam (hundreds of “craft X” ticks), vanilla Minecraft tabs, and mods with only a root stub (e.g. Mega Showdown).

    CobbleVerse tab (69)

    Pack progression: starter → Kanto → Johto → Hoenn → Sinnoh (gyms + Elite Four + Champion), then Kanto fossils / legendaries. Johto+ trees come from the PokeHaven EU datapack (toast-only — badges still drop from trainer loot). Post-game: Post-game and legendaries.
