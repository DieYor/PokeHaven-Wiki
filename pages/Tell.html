Tell

Tell — Psychic specialist on PokeHaven EU.

Contents

    Walkthrough — Alice to Tell

    Prepare

    Dark, Bug, and Ghost pressure Psychic — Dark-types ignore his STAB entirely. Dense biome, claim a rest stop near the gym.

    • Coverage: Dark, Bug, and Ghost pressure Psychic.
    • Leader party levels: about 60–62
    • Approx cap while this fight is next: ~67 — Level cap
    • Heals, status cures, spare balls, claimed base / waystone to retreat to
    • Amethyst Rainforest tip — dense biome, bring shears/an axe and claim a rest stop.

    Craft the map

    1. Search Tell in REI and craft Psychic Medallion.
    2. Craft a fresh Empty Map.
    3. Combine Empty Map + Psychic Medallion in the Hoenn Cartography Table (or trade a Map Guide villager).
    4. Hover the finished map for coordinates. Details: Gym maps.
    Important

    Do not open the Empty Map in the world first — that ruins it for gym crafting. Use the correct region cartography table.

    Fight tips

    Solrock, Lunatone, and Gardevoir hit hard on the special side. Dark-types ignore his Psychic STAB entirely.

    1. Travel with heals; activate waystones on the route.
    2. Clear gym trainers if you need XP or PokéDollars.
    3. Heal, then challenge Tell.
    4. Win → level cap rises → next: Adriano.
