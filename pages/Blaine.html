Blaine

Blaine — Fire specialist on PokeHaven EU.

Contents

    Walkthrough — Sabrina to Blaine

    Prepare

    Water and Ground are reliable. Prepare for Nether-adjacent biomes.

    • Coverage: Water and Ground are reliable into Fire.
    • Leader party levels: about 57–60
    • Approx cap while this fight is next: ~65 — Level cap
    • Heals, status cures, spare balls, claimed base / waystone to retreat to
    • Crimson Forest / Nether-adjacent tip — fire resist gear helps the trip.

    Craft the map

    1. Search Blaine in REI and craft Cinnabar Glasses.
    2. Craft a fresh Empty Map.
    3. Combine Empty Map + Cinnabar Glasses in the Kanto Cartography Table (or trade a Map Guide villager).
    4. Hover the finished map for coordinates. Details: Gym maps.
    Important

    Do not open the Empty Map in the world first — that ruins it for gym crafting. Use the correct region cartography table.

    Fight tips

    Hot biomes kill unprepared travellers. Waystones + food before the gym fight.

    1. Travel with heals; activate waystones on the route.
    2. Clear gym trainers if you need XP or PokéDollars.
    3. Heal, then challenge Blaine.
    4. Win → level cap rises → next: Giovanni.
