Breeding

Breed in a claimed pasture near your base. Eggs take several minutes — plan farms, not frantic AFK at spawn.

Contents

    Setup

    1. Claim the land with FTB Chunks so nobody loots parents or eggs.
    2. Build a small pasture / pen near your bed and waystone.
    3. Put a compatible pair (or Ditto + parent) in range of the pasture block.
    4. Wait for eggs — pack timing is often roughly 7–15 minutes per window, not instant.

    What to expect

    • Shiny odds can improve with Masuda-style pairs and charm / crystal methods the pack enables — still rare.
    • Hidden Abilities can pass when the pack allows it.
    • Some legendary / paradox / ultra beast Ditto chains may be blocked.
    Practical tip
    Breed after you have a stable gym team and balls farm. Early game: catch coverage first; breed for IV / shiny projects later.
