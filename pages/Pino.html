Pino

Pino — Psychic specialist on PokeHaven EU.

Contents

    Walkthrough — Sandra to Johto Elite Four (Pino)

    Prepare

    Dark, Bug, and Ghost pressure Psychic. Full heal before every Johto Elite room.

    • Coverage: Dark, Bug, and Ghost pressure Psychic.
    • Leader party levels: about 75–75
    • Approx cap while this fight is next: ~80 — Level cap
    • Heals, status cures, spare balls, claimed base / waystone to retreat to
    • Elite Four Tower (The End) — finish all eight Johto gyms; bring stacks of heals.

    Craft the map

    1. Search Pino in REI and craft Power Lens.
    2. Craft a fresh Empty Map.
    3. Combine Empty Map + Power Lens in the Johto Cartography Table (or trade a Map Guide villager).
    4. Hover the finished map for coordinates. Details: Gym maps.
    Important

    Do not open the Empty Map in the world first — that ruins it for gym crafting. Use the correct region cartography table.

    Fight tips

    Full heal between Elite rooms. Do not walk into Johto Koga half-dead.

    1. Travel with heals; activate waystones on the route.
    2. Elite Four / Champion: full heal between rooms.
    3. Heal, then challenge Pino.
    4. Win → level cap rises → next: Koga.
