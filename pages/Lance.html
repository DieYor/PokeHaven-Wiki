Lance

Lance — Dragon specialist on PokeHaven EU.

Contents

    Walkthrough — Agatha to Lance

    Prepare

    Ice and Dragon moves matter. Bring a balanced late-game team.

    • Coverage: Ice and Dragon matter most; Fairy also helps into Dragon.
    • Leader party levels: about 90–90
    • Approx cap while this fight is next: ~95 — Level cap
    • Heals, status cures, spare balls, claimed base / waystone to retreat to
    • Fourth Elite room — this is a late-Kanto wall.

    Craft the map

    1. Search Lance in REI and craft Dragon Fang.
    2. Craft a fresh Empty Map.
    3. Combine Empty Map + Dragon Fang in the Kanto Cartography Table (or trade a Map Guide villager).
    4. Hover the finished map for coordinates. Details: Gym maps.
    Important

    Do not open the Empty Map in the world first — that ruins it for gym crafting. Use the correct region cartography table.

    Fight tips

    Dragon spam punishes thin teams. Bring Ice coverage and multiple win conditions.

    1. Travel with heals; activate waystones on the route.
    2. Elite Four / Champion: full heal between rooms.
    3. Heal, then challenge Lance.
    4. Win → level cap rises → next: Blue.
