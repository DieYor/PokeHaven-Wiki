Haven Cases

Daily loot cases with a CS-style spin. One free case per day, extras for PokéDollars, and published drop odds so you know what you are rolling for.

Contents

    How it works

    • 1 free Haven Case per calendar day (timezone Europe/Amsterdam)
    • Auto-granted on join if you have inventory space, or claim with /havencase claim
    • Buy extras with /havencase buy (default 4000$ each) — check /havencase status
    • Right-click the case in your inventory to open the spin UI
    • The server rolls first; the spin only animates the result

    Rarity ladder

    • Common ~34.5%
    • Uncommon ~22.5%
    • Rare ~25.5%
    • Epic ~17% (server chat broadcast)
    • Legendary ~0.4% (server chat broadcast)
    • Mythic ~0.1% (server chat broadcast)

    Odds can change on balance patches. This page matches the published pool snapshot — if something feels wrong, post in Discord #bug-reports.

    FAQ

    • When does the free case reset? Midnight in Europe/Amsterdam.
    • Is there pity? No — every open is independent.
    • Can I get a wild legendary from a case? No.
    • Why did chat announce my win? Epic and Legendary wins are broadcast server-wide.
    • Where else can I get Leftovers? Eat vanilla apples (2.5% chance). Plantable apple trees (Gamemode 4) grow in forests / Wandering Trader saplings — harvested apples are normal minecraft:apple.
