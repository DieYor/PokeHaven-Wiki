Recipe browser

Search 559 recipes parsed from CobbleVerse datapacks (TMs, LumyMon, cuisine, …). Cobblemon core ball recipes are also covered in Essential recipes because those live in mod JARs, not this datapack dump.

    How to use
    Filter by name or ingredient. Click a row to expand the crafting grid when the recipe is shaped. In-game REI (E) remains the live authority if a pack update changes a craft.
