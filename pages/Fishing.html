Fishing

On CobbleVerse / PokeHaven EU you can fish up Pokémon — not only vanilla fish. Use Cobblemon rods in the right water biome.

Contents

    Why fish?

    Hundreds of species only (or mainly) appear through fishing spawns — Magikarp lines, Tentacool, many Water types, and rarer ocean/river finds. It is a real progression tool, not just AFK fluff.

    How to start

    1. Open inventory search (E / REI) and search rod or Poke Rod.
    2. Craft a Cobblemon fishing rod (e.g. Poke Rod, Lure Rod, Great Rod…). A plain Minecraft fishing rod is mainly for vanilla loot.
    3. Stand next to water that matches the biome you want (river / ocean / swamp tags matter).
    4. Cast, wait for the bite, reel in — a wild Pokémon battle / encounter can start instead of an item.
    5. Catch with balls as usual. Keep your claim nearby if you AFK near a personal dock.
    Important

    Use Cobblemon rods for Pokémon. If you only get sticks and pufferfish, you are probably on a vanilla rod or the wrong water/biome.

    Rods, lure level & bait

    • Rod tiers (Poke → Great → Ultra → Master, plus themed rods like Net / Dive / Lure) raise what the pack treats as lure level. Higher lure unlocks fishing rows that require minLureLevel 1–3+.
    • Themed rods (Net Rod, Dive Rod, Friend Rod, …) often mirror ball themes — craft what REI shows; loot chests can also drop damaged rods.
    • Bait exists in the pack — attach / use bait when the item tooltip allows it to bias bites. Check REI for “bait”.
    • You do not need a Pokémon in your party to fish on PokeHaven EU.

    Where to cast

    • Freshwater tags (rivers, lakes) vs ocean / beach tags change the pool — move biomes if you only see the same commons.
    • Some species also list submerged / surface spawns (swim/boat encounters). Fishing is the rod-only context.
    • Night, weather, and luck can still matter like other Cobblemon spawns — do not expect one dock forever.

    Look up fishable Pokémon

    Open Spawn lookup (fishing filter), optionally filter biome text (e.g. ocean or freshwater).

    Examples you will see in pack data: Magikarp, Tentacool, Psyduck, Squirtle-line (rare), and many regional Water types — always verify with the lookup for your target.

    Gym tip
    Fishing is great for Water coverage before Misty / later oceans, but the level cap still applies — do not overlevel while grinding bites.
