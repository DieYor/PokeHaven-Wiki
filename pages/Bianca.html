Bianca

Bianca — Ice specialist on PokeHaven EU.

Contents

    Walkthrough — Ferruccio to Bianca

    Prepare

    Fire, Fighting, Rock, and Steel help into Ice. Weavile / Mamoswine hit hard physically — don't walk in underleveled.

    • Coverage: Fire, Fighting, Rock, and Steel help into Ice.
    • Leader party levels: about 66–67
    • Approx cap while this fight is next: ~72 — Level cap
    • Heals, status cures, spare balls, claimed base / waystone to retreat to
    • Glacial Chasm tip — cold-weather food and gear, this hike is long.

    Craft the map

    1. Search Bianca in REI and craft Ice Ribbon.
    2. Craft a fresh Empty Map.
    3. Combine Empty Map + Ice Ribbon in the Sinnoh Cartography Table (or trade a Map Guide villager).
    4. Hover the finished map for coordinates. Details: Gym maps.
    Important

    Do not open the Empty Map in the world first — that ruins it for gym crafting. Use the correct region cartography table.

    Fight tips

    Weavile / Mamoswine hit hard on the physical side; don’t walk in underleveled.

    1. Travel with heals; activate waystones on the route.
    2. Clear gym trainers if you need XP or PokéDollars.
    3. Heal, then challenge Bianca.
    4. Win → level cap rises → next: Corrado.
