Aaron

Aaron — Bug specialist on PokeHaven EU.

Contents

    Walkthrough — Corrado to Sinnoh Elite Four (Aaron)

    Prepare

    Fire, Flying, and Rock pressure Bug. Full heal before every Sinnoh Elite Four room — Aaron opens the gauntlet.

    • Coverage: Fire, Flying, and Rock pressure Bug.
    • Leader party levels: about 82–82
    • Approx cap while this fight is next: ~87 — Level cap
    • Heals, status cures, spare balls, claimed base / waystone to retreat to
    • Desert Oasis — the Sinnoh league grounds, not The End like Kanto/Johto.

    Craft the map

    1. Search Aaron in REI and craft Bug Net.
    2. Craft a fresh Empty Map.
    3. Combine Empty Map + Bug Net in the Sinnoh Cartography Table (or trade a Map Guide villager).
    4. Hover the finished map for coordinates. Details: Gym maps.
    Important

    Do not open the Empty Map in the world first — that ruins it for gym crafting. Use the correct region cartography table.

    Fight tips

    Full heal before every Sinnoh Elite Four room. Aaron opens the gauntlet.

    1. Travel with heals; activate waystones on the route.
    2. Elite Four / Champion: full heal between rooms.
    3. Heal, then challenge Aaron.
    4. Win → level cap rises → next: Terrie.
