Jasmine

Jasmine — Steel specialist. Team data from the CobbleVerse RCT datapack used on PokeHaven EU.

Contents

    Walkthrough — Furio to Jasmine

    Prepare

    Fire, Fighting, and Ground crack Steel. Magnezone / Metagross hit hard — don’t send pure Water blindly.

    • Coverage: Fire, Fighting, and Ground crack Steel.
    • Leader party levels (pack data): about 58–60
    • Approx cap while this fight is next: ~65 — Level cap
    • Heals, status cures, spare balls, claimed base / waystone to retreat to
    • Taiga tip — longer hike; restock balls and Revives.

    Craft the map

    1. Search Jasmine in REI and craft Secret Medicine.
    2. Craft a fresh Empty Map.
    3. Combine Empty Map + Secret Medicine in the Johto Cartography Table (or trade a Map Guide villager).
    4. Hover the finished map for coordinates. Details: Gym maps.
    Important

    Do not open the Empty Map in the world first — that ruins it for gym crafting. Use the correct region cartography table.

    Fight tips

    Magnezone / Metagross punish pure Water. Bring Ground or Fire that isn’t weak to Steel moves.

    1. Travel with heals; activate waystones on the route.
    2. Clear gym trainers if you need XP or CobbleDollars.
    3. Heal, then challenge Jasmine.
    4. Win → level cap rises → next: Alfredo.
