Bruno

Bruno — Fighting specialist on PokeHaven EU.

Contents

    Walkthrough — Lorelei to Bruno

    Prepare

    Flying, Psychic, and Fairy are strong answers.

    • Coverage: Flying, Psychic, and Fairy answer Fighting.
    • Leader party levels: about 80–80
    • Approx cap while this fight is next: ~85 — Level cap
    • Heals, status cures, spare balls, claimed base / waystone to retreat to
    • Same Elite Four Tower — restock before you enter his room.

    Craft the map

    1. Search Bruno in REI and craft Focus Band.
    2. Craft a fresh Empty Map.
    3. Combine Empty Map + Focus Band in the Kanto Cartography Table (or trade a Map Guide villager).
    4. Hover the finished map for coordinates. Details: Gym maps.
    Important

    Do not open the Empty Map in the world first — that ruins it for gym crafting. Use the correct region cartography table.

    Fight tips

    Fighting hits Normal/Rock/Ice/Steel hard. Keep a Flying/Psychic pivot.

    1. Travel with heals; activate waystones on the route.
    2. Elite Four / Champion: full heal between rooms.
    3. Heal, then challenge Bruno.
    4. Win → level cap rises → next: Agatha.
