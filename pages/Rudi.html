Rudi

Rudi — Fighting specialist on PokeHaven EU.

Contents

    Walkthrough — Petra to Rudi

    Prepare

    Flying, Psychic, and Fairy answer Fighting. This is a vertical map — bring blocks or a Flying mount for the ledges.

    • Coverage: Flying, Psychic, and Fairy answer Fighting.
    • Leader party levels: about 27–30
    • Approx cap while this fight is next: ~35 — Level cap
    • Heals, status cures, spare balls, claimed base / waystone to retreat to
    • Yosemite Cliffs tip — this is a vertical map; bring blocks or a Flying mount for the ledges.

    Craft the map

    1. Search Rudi in REI and craft Fighting Glove.
    2. Craft a fresh Empty Map.
    3. Combine Empty Map + Fighting Glove in the Hoenn Cartography Table (or trade a Map Guide villager).
    4. Hover the finished map for coordinates. Details: Gym maps.
    Important

    Do not open the Empty Map in the world first — that ruins it for gym crafting. Use the correct region cartography table.

    Fight tips

    Fighting hits Normal/Rock/Ice/Steel/Dark hard. Don’t walk in with a mono-Rock team from Petra’s fight.

    1. Travel with heals; activate waystones on the route.
    2. Clear gym trainers if you need XP or PokéDollars.
    3. Heal, then challenge Rudi.
    4. Win → level cap rises → next: Walter.
