Blue

Blue — Mixed specialist. Team data from the CobbleVerse RCT datapack used on PokeHaven EU.

Contents

    Walkthrough — Lance to Champion Blue

    Prepare

    Cover many types. Unique drop notes mention Ancient Origin Ball on some packs.

    • Coverage: Mixed champion — pack answers for several types, not one gimmick.
    • Leader party levels (pack data): about 100–100
    • Approx cap while this fight is next: ~105 — Level cap
    • Heals, status cures, spare balls, claimed base / waystone to retreat to
    • Top of the Elite Four Tower. Full restore team + items.

    Craft the map

    1. Search Blue in REI and craft Rival Sling.
    2. Craft a fresh Empty Map.
    3. Combine Empty Map + Rival Sling in the Kanto Cartography Table (or trade a Map Guide villager).
    4. Hover the finished map for coordinates. Details: Gym maps.
    Important

    Do not open the Empty Map in the world first — that ruins it for gym crafting.

    Fight tips

    After Blue on PokeHaven: Johto unlock + Trainer Association card swap. Soft Discord note if structures are missing (staff may restart once). See your champion book.

    1. Travel with heals; activate waystones on the route.
    2. Elite Four / Champion: full heal between rooms.
    3. Heal, then challenge Blue.
    4. Win → Kanto Champion. Next: Johto via Trainer Association (cap resets for you). Details: Progression · Johto.
