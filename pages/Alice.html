Alice

Alice — Flying specialist on PokeHaven EU.

Contents

    Walkthrough — Norman to Alice

    Prepare

    Electric, Rock, and Ice punish Flying hard. Dim biome — bring light sources and food for the trip.

    • Coverage: Electric, Rock, and Ice punish Flying hard.
    • Leader party levels: about 52–56
    • Approx cap while this fight is next: ~61 — Level cap
    • Heals, status cures, spare balls, claimed base / waystone to retreat to
    • Moonlight Grove tip — a dim biome; bring light sources and food.

    Craft the map

    1. Search Alice in REI and craft Flying Feather.
    2. Craft a fresh Empty Map.
    3. Combine Empty Map + Flying Feather in the Hoenn Cartography Table (or trade a Map Guide villager).
    4. Hover the finished map for coordinates. Details: Gym maps.
    Important

    Do not open the Empty Map in the world first — that ruins it for gym crafting. Use the correct region cartography table.

    Fight tips

    Altaria and Skarmory can tank hits. Electric and Rock coverage ends this fast.

    1. Travel with heals; activate waystones on the route.
    2. Clear gym trainers if you need XP or PokéDollars.
    3. Heal, then challenge Alice.
    4. Win → level cap rises → next: Tell.
