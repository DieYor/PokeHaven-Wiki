Sinnoh gyms

Sinnoh opens after Hoenn Champion Rocco. Gym maps, opener Pedro, and the trainer list below.

Contents

    Unlock

    1. Beat Hoenn Champion Rocco (after the Hoenn league).
    2. Follow your Trainer Card unlocks into Sinnoh.
    3. Use the Sinnoh Cartography Table — Gym maps. First leader: Pedro.
